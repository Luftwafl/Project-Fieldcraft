Project Fieldcraft - BETA 0.4.5 - test report

Changes in 0.4.5 (damage system)
- Hits land on a place: head, chest, arm or leg. A blast is spread.
- Helmet stops the first 3 pistol rounds to the head. Rifle rounds go through; a rifle head shot kills.
- Vest stops the first 4 rifle rounds to the chest. A chest round that gets through is a chest wound (20 blood, bleeds 1.5/s).
- Arm and leg wounds bleed (0.6/s each, 8 blood per rifle hit, 4 per pistol hit).
- Blood is 100. At 50 the player is downed for 60 s. Bleeding continues while downed; a hit while downed is fatal; GIVE UP ends it.
- Revive: a teammate holds MED next to a downed teammate for 5 s and uses one of their kits. Revived at 50 blood with wounds still open. Multiplayer only.
- Medkits: 0 to 6 chosen on the role menu (default 2). MED treats the most severe wound, one kit per wound.
- Downed screen (countdown ring, revive ring, nearest teammate, GIVE UP) and death screen (wounds figure, hits, armour stops, cause, killer).
- Server relays hit location and weapon, the downed flag and revive progress. Version 0.4.5.

Results (headless Chromium, software GL)
- dmg45: helmet 3 stops then the 4th pistol head shot kills; rifle head kills; vest stops 4 then wounds; pistol chest not stopped by vest; leg wound leaves 92 blood; downed at 47, 60 s timer, bled out; kits take the most severe wound first; no kits and no wounds handled; from-behind flag; death screen counts hits.
- stage18 ALL PASS (death screen dismissed with CONTINUE first); npc18 errors none; npc18b zones head 4, vest 10; uni18 no errors; ai17_test 10/10.
- server/server.js syntax checked. Revive between two live clients not run.

Open items
- Single-player has no reviver: a downed player bleeds out unless the rules are extended to AI squadmates.
- Enemy NPC soldiers fire rifles only, so helmets matter against pistols only in PvP.
- NPC soldiers do not use the helmet or vest rules; their damage model is unchanged.
- Touch layout of the MED button (bottom right) not checked on a phone.
- Performance on a real GPU and phone not checked.
